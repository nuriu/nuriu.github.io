Numera Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Numera handles information. It applies to the Numera iOS app, including the embedded Numera Widget extension and TestFlight beta builds.

For this policy, “Numera,” “we,” “us,” or “our” means the developer of Numera. You can contact us at numera@nuriu.dev.

Summary

Numera is designed to be private and on-device.

  • Numera does not require an account.
  • Numera does not use advertising.
  • Numera does not use third-party analytics.
  • Numera does not sell or share personal information.
  • Numera does not sync your counters to our servers.
  • Numera does not collect your counter content from the app.

Information stored on your device

Numera lets you create counters, countdowns, count-ups, recurring counters, widgets, completion alerts, and built-in counter pack items.

The app may store the information you enter or configure, such as:

  • counter titles;
  • dates and times;
  • recurrence settings;
  • selected icons, colors, layout preferences, and language preferences;
  • notes or other counter details you choose to enter;
  • completion-alert preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. The Numera Widget reads the counter information it needs through a shared App Group container so that your widgets can display your counters. We do not receive this information.

Your device, operating system, Apple ID settings, backups, or other Apple services may handle local app data according to your own Apple settings and Apple’s policies. For example, if you enable device backups, Apple may process backup data as part of that service. We do not control Apple’s systems.

Notifications and completion alerts

If you enable completion alerts, Numera uses Apple’s local notification system to schedule alerts on your device. These alerts are created and delivered by the device operating system. Numera does not send your counter data to our servers to provide these alerts.

Notification delivery is controlled by iOS settings and is not guaranteed.

TestFlight beta information

If you install Numera through TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs, diagnostic information, tester information, and feedback according to Apple’s TestFlight and privacy terms. If you submit feedback, screenshots, or crash information through TestFlight, Apple may make that information available to us so we can investigate bugs and improve the app.

Do not include sensitive personal information in TestFlight feedback unless it is necessary for your support request.

Support email

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, device details included by your email app, and any app details you include. We use support emails only to answer your request, investigate issues, improve Numera, and keep necessary records.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your support request.

Website logs

If you visit this Privacy Policy or other Numera web pages, the website hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and similar server-log data for security, delivery, diagnostics, and abuse prevention. The current Numera legal pages are not used for advertising or analytics.

No sale, sharing, advertising, or tracking

Numera does not sell personal information. Numera does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Numera does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Third-party services

Numera does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, account systems, or cloud synchronization services.

Numera is distributed through Apple services such as the App Store and TestFlight. Apple processes information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

Children

Numera is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email or feedback, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

You control the information stored in Numera:

  • You can create, edit, or delete counters in the app.
  • You can disable completion alerts in the app or in iOS settings.
  • You can remove widgets from your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  • You can delete Numera from your device to remove locally stored app data, subject to your device backups and Apple settings.
  • You can contact us at numera@nuriu.dev about privacy questions or support email records.

Because Numera does not maintain user accounts or server-side counter data, we generally cannot access, export, or delete counter data stored only on your device.

International users

Numera may be available to users in multiple countries or regions. Support emails and TestFlight feedback may be processed where we or our service providers operate. We use the information you voluntarily send only for the purposes described in this policy.

Future features

Numera may add optional features in the future, such as cloud synchronization, accounts, analytics, or subscriptions. If a future version changes how data is collected, used, or shared, we will update this Privacy Policy and App Store privacy disclosures before or when those changes apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be reflected in the published policy and, where appropriate, in the app or App Store listing.
