Numera Support

Help for Numera on iPhone and iPad

Numera helps you create Countdowns, Count-ups, Recurring Counters, Home Screen Widgets, Completion Alerts, and Counters from built-in Counter Packs.

Contact

Email numera@nuriu.dev for help, feedback, or privacy questions. English and Turkish messages are welcome.

When reporting a problem, include your Numera version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the request.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a Counter?

Tap the add button on the Home Dashboard, choose a Counter Direction and Counter Recurrence, then customize the title, date, icon, color, notes, and eligible Completion Alert preference.

Where is my Counter data stored?

Counter data is stored locally on your device. Numera does not require an account and does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization. The embedded Widget reads the same on-device data through an App Group.

Why did a Completion Alert not appear?

Completion Alerts are optional local notifications. Confirm that notifications are allowed for Numera in iOS Settings, the global Completion Alerts setting is on, and the individual eligible Countdown has alerts enabled. Delivery is controlled by iOS and is not guaranteed.

How do I add a Widget?

From the iOS or iPadOS Home Screen, open the system Widget gallery, search for Numera, choose a supported size, and add it. Where available, edit the Widget to select the Counter it should show.

How do I remove my data?

You can delete individual Counters or use Numera's reset option. Deleting the app removes its local data subject to your device backups and Apple settings.
